Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Blood Connect ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Blood Connect mobile application ("App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our services.

By using Blood Connect, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Blood Connect, we may collect the following information.

A. Account Information

When you create an account or use our login functionality, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password or authentication-related information
  • Date of birth or age, where required
  • Blood group
  • Donor-related information
  • Profile information

B. Location Information

Blood Connect may request access to your device's location to provide location-based functionality, such as:

  • Finding nearby blood donors
  • Displaying donors based on distance
  • Helping users identify nearby blood donation resources
  • Improving location-based search results

Location access is requested only when required by the relevant feature.

You can control location permissions through your device settings.

C. Profile Photo and Camera

If you choose to add or update your profile photo, Blood Connect may request access to your device camera or photos.

We use this information only for the relevant profile functionality.

D. Blood Donation and Request Information

When you use blood donation or blood request features, we may collect information such as:

  • Blood group
  • Donation availability
  • Blood request details
  • Required blood group
  • Location associated with a request
  • Request status
  • Other information you voluntarily provide

Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive medical information through the App.

E. Device and Technical Information

We may automatically receive limited technical information when you use the App, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Device identifiers or installation identifiers
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Network-related information

This information may be used to maintain, secure, and improve the App.

F. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Blood Connect may send notifications related to:

  • Blood requests
  • Donor-related activities
  • Account activity
  • Important service notifications
  • Updates related to the App

You can disable notifications through your device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide blood donor and blood request services
  • Help users find nearby donors
  • Process and manage blood requests
  • Display relevant donor information
  • Communicate with users
  • Send notifications
  • Improve App functionality
  • Monitor and troubleshoot technical problems
  • Protect the security and integrity of the App
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

3. How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary to provide the App's functionality, including:

  • With other Blood Connect users when required for donor/request functionality.
  • With service providers that help us operate the App.
  • With Firebase and other technical service providers used by the App.
  • When required by law or a valid legal process.
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service.

For example, information that you choose to make available as part of a donor profile may be visible to other users when they use donor-search functionality.

4. Firebase and Third-Party Services

Blood Connect may use third-party services such as Firebase to support application functionality, authentication, databases, notifications, analytics, crash reporting, or other technical services.

These services may process certain information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with our applicable retention practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Blood Connect is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

9. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for ensuring that the information you provide through Blood Connect is accurate and that you have the necessary permission to provide information about another person.

You should not use the App to submit or distribute unlawful, fraudulent, or unnecessary sensitive information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

When we make changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.
