Blood Connect - Account Deletion Request

If you would like to delete your Blood Connect account and associated personal data, you can submit an account deletion request using the contact information below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email from your registered email address with the subject "Blood Connect Account Deletion Request".

Please include the email address associated with your Blood Connect account so that we can identify your account and process the request.

What Happens After Your Request

After receiving your request, ClubTravalet will verify the request and process the deletion of your account and associated personal data, subject to any information that we are legally required to retain.
